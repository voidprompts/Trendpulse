Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit TrendPulse (the "Site"), how it is used, and the choices available to you. By using the Site you agree to the practices described below.

1. Who we are

TrendPulse is an independent, automatically generated website that publishes trending social media topics and matching hashtag suggestions. The Site is static: it has no user accounts, no login system, and no database of visitors. Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact address listed in section 11.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, postal address, or telephone number. There are no registration forms, newsletters, or comment sections on this Site.

Two categories of data are nevertheless processed automatically:

  • Server log data. Our hosting provider records standard technical information for every request, including IP address, browser type, operating system, referring page, and timestamp. This is used for security, abuse prevention, and aggregate traffic measurement.
  • Cookie and advertising data. Third-party partners described in section 4 may place cookies or similar identifiers in your browser.

Hashtag copying happens entirely inside your browser using the native Clipboard API. The content you copy is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

3. Cookies

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device by your web browser. TrendPulse itself does not set first-party tracking cookies. However, advertising and analytics vendors that operate on this Site may set their own cookies to measure performance and to deliver and personalise advertisements.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Most browsers allow you to block third-party cookies entirely. Disabling cookies will not prevent you from using this Site, although the advertisements you see may become less relevant.

4. Third-party advertising vendors

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, users may opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices, or the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page at optout.networkadvertising.org.
  • If you have not opted out of third-party ad serving, the cookies of other third-party vendors or ad networks may also be used to serve ads on this Site.

For more detail on how Google handles data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns, such as which pages are most visited and which countries visitors come from. This information is reported in aggregate and is not used to identify individual visitors.

6. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data under laws such as the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). These may include the right to access, correct, or delete your data, and the right to object to certain processing.

Because TrendPulse does not collect personal information directly, most such requests should be directed to the relevant advertising or hosting vendor. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, our advertising partners are required to obtain consent for personalised advertising before setting non-essential cookies, and you may withdraw that consent at any time through the consent prompt or your browser settings.

7. Children's privacy

This Site is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through this Site, please contact us so it can be removed.

8. External links

TrendPulse may link to third-party websites, and advertisements displayed on the Site will lead to external destinations. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of any site you visit after leaving TrendPulse.

9. Data security

The Site is served as static files over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Because we do not operate a database or store user submissions, the risk surface is minimal. No method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure, however, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in applicable law. Any revisions take effect as soon as they are published on this page, and the "Last updated" date above will be revised accordingly. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

11. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at contact@trendpulse.example.
